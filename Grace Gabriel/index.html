Snow Mountain Arcade

Match holiday cards or take the perfect photo of a snowy scene.

Memory Match

Find matching holiday symbols on a board of 16 cards.

How to play: Flip two cards at a time. Matching cards stay face up. Other cards turn back over.

How to win: Find all eight pairs. Each match earns 10 points.

Snow Shot

Choose one of five snow scenes and adjust the camera exposure.

How to play: Start at 0. Use +4, +2, or −1 to change the exposure. Each click uses one move.

How to win: Reach 7 in three moves or fewer, then click Take Photo.
